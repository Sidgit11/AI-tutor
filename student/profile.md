# Student profile — Siddhant

## Who

**Siddhant** — 0→1 product manager and founder. Building AI SaaS in trade intelligence.

## Baseline — the honest version

**Ships with AI assistance.** Can build working systems with an AI pair: the products exist, they run, people use them. That is a real capability and this course does not pretend otherwise.

The gap is what happens when the assistant is removed. The target is competence that survives an **unassisted screen** — the Stripe formulation, verbatim, which is the sentence this whole course is built around:

> *"fluent with AI-assisted development but not dependent on it."*

Both halves are requirements. The goal is not to stop using AI assistance; it is to stop *needing* it. So the course tracks `mode_counts` and deliberately shifts the assisted/unassisted ratio: heavy on unassisted reps early, back toward assisted later, because that ratio is the thing actually being trained.

## Pace

**5–10 hours per week, in 60–90 minute blocks.**

This is a founder's schedule, not a student's. It bends around a company. The design consequences are structural, not motivational:

- Sessions are crash-safe — a session killed by a meeting or a kid costs nothing, because `state.json` checkpoints at every phase boundary.
- A 20-minute session is a legitimate session. `/standup 20` produces real work, not a guilt trip.
- Timeboxes are hard. At ~90% of the planned budget the tutor lands the plane; unfinished threads go to the parking lot and get pre-loaded into the next session.
- Streaks freeze for declared breaks. They never reset punitively.

Gates are **mastery gates, not time gates**. A bad month delays the calendar and moves nothing else.

## Target roles

- **Applied / Product AI Engineer**
- **Forward-Deployed Engineer**

Both are shipping roles rather than research roles — which is why the curriculum is weighted toward evals, retrieval, agents, and production concerns rather than model internals, and why every module ends in something deployed.

## North star

**To be declared at intake (end of Module 0.)**

The course is designed to compile itself against a declared north-star product — the agent you actually want to exist. Once declared, every module project becomes a milestone of *that* product rather than a generic exercise: Module 1's CRUD service is its backend, Module 4's retrieval runs over its corpus, Module 8 launches it to a real user.

Until intake, module projects run generically. Intake is owned by the director (staged, v0.3); in the meantime the declaration goes in the journal at Module 0 close — target user, core job-to-be-done, tool surface, data sources, and one success metric.

Roughly **70/30**: module projects thread through the north star; reps and transfer exercises stay deliberately generic and disposable, so what gets learned is the discipline and not just one codebase.

## How to work with me

From the constitution, and it means it: **direct, no filler, no cheerleading.** Push back when I'm wrong. Announce the mode at every switch — ambiguity about whether AI help is allowed is a design failure. Openings are two lines; the journal is the recap.

Celebrate a passed gate in one sentence. Dissect a failed one for as long as it takes.
